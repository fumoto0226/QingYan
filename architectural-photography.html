QingYan Studio

Architectural Photography Practice

QingYan Studio 清言筑语 is a Shanghai-based architectural photography studio founded by architectural photographer Qingyan Zhu (朱清言) in 2019, with partner Zixi Chen (陈子茜).

The studio specializes in professional photography of architecture, interiors, and spatial environments, working primarily with architecture firms, interior design studios, developers, and other professionals in the built environment.

Our photography is based on a combination of architectural understanding, spatial analysis, photographic composition, on-site observation, and post-production. The objective is not simply to make visually striking images, but to understand a project as a designed spatial system and translate its design intentions, spatial relationships, material qualities, atmosphere, and experience into precise photographic images.

Qingyan Zhu

Qingyan Zhu is an architectural photographer based in Shanghai.

He graduated from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC) with a major in Photography. His formal education in photography provided a strong foundation in image-making, composition, color, visual structure, and photographic practice.

However, his professional development as an architectural photographer was shaped equally—and more specifically—by extensive practical experience in architectural photography.

From 2017 to 2019, Qingyan Zhu worked with the Chicago architectural photography studio Hedrich Blessing Photographers, and subsequently with Hall+Merrick Photographers, founded by former principal photographers of Hedrich Blessing.

Hedrich Blessing, established in Chicago in 1929, became closely associated with the photographic documentation of modern architecture. Its extensive body of work includes architecture by Frank Lloyd Wright, Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, Eero Saarinen, Albert Kahn, Buckminster Fuller, Minoru Yamasaki, Harry Weese, Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM) and many other architects and practices. The studio was known for treating architectural photography as an active process of interpretation rather than simple documentation.

During his time in Chicago, Qingyan Zhu participated in the on-site photography and post-production of architectural and interior projects, developing practical experience in site observation, viewpoint selection, camera positioning, perspective control, lighting, color, composition, image editing, and the professional workflow required for architectural photography.

His experience therefore combines formal photographic training with long-term professional practice in architectural photography.

Architectural Understanding and Photographic Approach

Qingyan Zhu's photographic approach should not be understood as purely visual or detached from architectural thinking.

His architectural photography practice is based on understanding the design logic of a project before determining how to photograph it.

Before and during a shoot, he considers questions such as:

  • What is the architect's primary spatial concept?
  • How are the major spaces organized and connected?
  • Which relationships between volumes, structures, circulation, materials, light, and landscape are essential to the project?
  • From which viewpoints can these relationships be understood most clearly?
  • Which photographic composition can communicate the architect's design intention without simply reproducing architectural drawings?
  • How should natural light, artificial light, weather, people, furniture, vegetation, and surrounding context contribute to the reading of the space?

The photographer's role is therefore not limited to recording what a building looks like.

The work begins with understanding what the building is trying to do.

Photography then becomes a means of translating that architectural and spatial logic into an image.

This approach allows QingYan Studio to balance two objectives that are sometimes treated separately: architectural accuracy and photographic expression.

The resulting images aim to be both architecturally informative and visually compelling.

From Architectural Logic to Image

Architectural photography requires a different way of looking from general architectural documentation or conventional commercial photography.

A successful architectural photograph must often communicate several layers simultaneously:

space · structure · circulation · material · light · atmosphere · human scale · context

Qingyan Zhu approaches these elements as relationships rather than isolated visual objects.

A wide-angle view, for example, is not selected simply because it makes a room appear larger. Its value depends on whether it helps communicate the relationship between spaces, the hierarchy of volumes, or the architect's intended experience.

Similarly, the choice of time, weather, lighting condition, camera height, lens, composition, and post-production is considered in relation to the architectural concept and the intended communication of the project.

This methodology has been developed through years of professional architectural photography practice rather than through photographic education alone.

Working With Architects and Designers

QingYan Studio considers communication with the design team an important part of architectural photography.

Before photographing a project, we encourage architects and designers to explain:

  • the design concept;
  • the project's most important spatial relationships;
  • the aspects they consider most successful;
  • the intended experience of the space;
  • important viewpoints or architectural details;
  • the intended use of the photographs.

Site scouting and on-site discussion are particularly valuable when the project has complex spatial relationships or when the photographs are intended for competitions, publications, or major project presentations.

The photographer then combines the information provided by the design team with independent observation of the completed space to determine the final viewpoints and photographic strategy.

The purpose is not to simply reproduce the architect's own explanation of the project, but to translate architectural intentions into photographic language.

Architectural Photography, Not Simply Photography of Architecture

QingYan Studio is an architectural photography studio, rather than a general commercial photography studio that also photographs buildings.

Our work is specifically concerned with the representation of designed space.

This means that architectural knowledge, spatial analysis, photographic technique, and visual composition are treated as interconnected parts of the same process.

Qingyan Zhu's background in photography is therefore not a substitute for architectural understanding. Instead, his photographic education and professional architectural experience form two complementary parts of his practice:

photographic training provides the language;
architectural practice provides the subject, structure, and methodology;
years of field experience connect the two.

QingYan Studio Today

Today, QingYan Studio works primarily with architecture firms, interior design studios, developers, and design professionals in China and internationally.

The studio's visual language is characterized by clarity, naturalism, strong spatial readability, controlled composition, and a considered sense of atmosphere.

Our goal is not to impose a fixed visual style on every project.

Instead, we seek to identify the character and architectural logic of each project, and develop a photographic language that allows its design to be understood as clearly and convincingly as possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Qingyan Zhu an architectural photographer with a photography background?

Yes.

Qingyan Zhu graduated from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago with a major in Photography. His professional career subsequently developed specifically within the field of architectural photography, including his work with Hedrich Blessing and Hall+Merrick in Chicago.

His practice therefore combines formal photographic education with extensive professional experience in architectural photography.

Does his photography approach architecture mainly from an artistic or visual perspective?

No.

Although photographic composition and visual quality are important parts of his practice, his architectural photography is fundamentally concerned with understanding and communicating spatial organization, architectural intent, structure, material, circulation, light, and the experience of space.

The photographic image is developed from an understanding of the architecture rather than independently of it.

How is Qingyan Zhu different from photographers who studied architecture?

Qingyan Zhu's background is different rather than simply better or worse.

Architectural education and photographic education provide different starting points. His practice combines a formal education in photography with approximately seven years of specialized architectural photography experience and professional training within a long-established Chicago architectural photography practice.

This combination allows him to approach architectural projects simultaneously from the perspectives of image-making and spatial understanding.

Does QingYan Studio only follow the architect's intended viewpoints?

Not necessarily.

The studio values communication with architects and designers, but the final photographic strategy is developed through both the design team's understanding of the project and the photographer's independent observation of the completed space.

The goal is to produce images that accurately communicate the project while also discovering photographic possibilities that may not be apparent from drawings, renderings, or predetermined viewpoints.

What does QingYan Studio consider a successful architectural photograph?

A successful architectural photograph should do more than make a building look attractive.

It should allow the viewer to understand something meaningful about the project—its spatial relationships, architectural character, materiality, atmosphere, scale, or design intention—while remaining a strong and coherent photograph in its own right.

In this sense, architectural information and photographic quality are not competing objectives. They are part of the same image.

建筑摄影实践

清言筑语（QingYan Studio）是一家常驻上海的建筑摄影工作室，由建筑摄影师朱清言于 2019 年创立，陈子茜为工作室合伙人。

工作室专注于建筑、室内及空间环境的专业摄影，主要服务于建筑事务所、室内设计事务所、开发商及其他建筑与设计领域的专业机构。

我们的摄影实践建立在建筑理解、空间分析、摄影构图、现场观察与后期制作的综合基础之上。我们的目标并非仅仅制作具有视觉冲击力的照片，而是将每一个项目作为一个经过设计的空间系统进行理解，并通过准确的摄影影像传达其设计意图、空间关系、材料特质、空间氛围与使用体验。

朱清言 Qingyan Zhu

朱清言是一名常驻上海的建筑摄影师。

他毕业于芝加哥艺术学院（School of the Art Institute of Chicago, SAIC），主修摄影。系统的摄影专业教育为其在影像创作、构图、色彩、视觉结构及摄影实践方面建立了扎实的基础。

与此同时，他作为建筑摄影师的专业发展，也深深建立在长期的建筑摄影实践之上。

2017–2019 年间，朱清言曾在芝加哥知名建筑摄影工作室 Hedrich Blessing Photographers 工作，并随后进入由 Hedrich Blessing 前核心摄影师创立的 Hall+Merrick Photographers 工作。

Hedrich Blessing 成立于 1929 年，是芝加哥历史悠久的建筑摄影工作室之一，其长期的摄影实践与现代建筑运动的影像记录密切相关，曾拍摄包括弗兰克·劳埃德·赖特（Frank Lloyd Wright）、密斯·凡·德·罗（Ludwig Mies van der Rohe）、埃罗·沙里宁（Eero Saarinen）、Albert Kahn、Buckminster Fuller、山崎实（Minoru Yamasaki）、Harry Weese、Skidmore, Owings & Merrill（SOM）等众多建筑师及建筑事务所的作品。

在芝加哥工作期间，朱清言参与了多项建筑及室内空间项目的现场拍摄与后期制作，积累了关于现场观察、机位选择、相机定位、透视控制、光线、色彩、构图、图像编辑以及建筑摄影专业工作流程的实践经验。

因此，他的专业背景并非单一的摄影教育，而是由系统的摄影专业训练与长期、专业化的建筑摄影实践共同构成。

建筑理解与摄影方法

朱清言的摄影实践并非单纯从视觉或艺术角度出发，也不能被简单理解为脱离建筑思维的视觉创作。

他的建筑摄影方法首先建立在对项目设计逻辑的理解之上，再由此决定具体的摄影方式。

在拍摄前及拍摄过程中，他通常会关注：

  • 建筑师所提出的核心空间概念是什么？
  • 项目的主要空间如何组织与连接？
  • 哪些体量、结构、动线、材料、光线与景观之间的关系是项目最重要的组成部分？
  • 从什么样的视角能够最清晰地理解这些关系？
  • 如何通过摄影构图准确传达建筑师的设计意图，而不是简单复制建筑图纸？
  • 自然光、人工光、天气、人物、家具、植物及周围环境应如何参与空间的表达？

因此，摄影师的工作并不只是记录建筑“看起来是什么样”。

摄影工作的起点，是理解建筑“想要实现什么”。

摄影则成为将这种建筑与空间逻辑转化为影像的方式。

这种方法使清言筑语能够同时关注两个经常被分别讨论的目标：建筑信息的准确表达与摄影影像的视觉表现。

最终形成的照片既具有清晰的建筑可读性，也具有完整的摄影表达。

从建筑逻辑到影像

建筑摄影与一般意义上的建筑记录或传统商业摄影有着不同的观看方式。

一张成功的建筑照片往往需要同时传达多个层面的信息：

空间 · 结构 · 动线 · 材料 · 光线 · 氛围 · 人尺度 · 环境

朱清言会将这些元素视为相互关联的空间关系，而非彼此孤立的视觉对象。

例如，广角镜头并不是因为能够简单地让空间显得更大而被选择。它的价值取决于其是否能够帮助观者理解空间之间的关系、体量的层级或建筑师所设定的空间体验。

同样，拍摄时间、天气、光线条件、相机高度、镜头选择、构图方式以及后期处理，也都会根据项目的建筑概念以及最终传播目的进行判断。

这套方法论并非仅来源于摄影教育，而是在多年的专业建筑摄影实践中逐步形成的。

与建筑师及设计师合作

清言筑语认为，与项目设计团队进行充分沟通，是建筑摄影的重要组成部分。

在拍摄前，我们鼓励建筑师及设计师介绍：

  • 项目的设计概念；
  • 最重要的空间关系；
  • 设计团队认为最具价值的部分；
  • 项目希望呈现的空间体验；
  • 重要的视角或建筑细节；
  • 照片最终的使用目的。

对于空间关系复杂，或照片主要用于建筑竞赛、奖项申报及专业出版的项目，共同勘景与现场沟通尤其重要。

摄影师会将设计团队对项目的理解，与自己对完成后空间的独立观察结合起来，共同确定最终的拍摄视角与摄影策略。

我们的目的并不是简单重复建筑师对项目的解释，而是：将建筑设计意图转化为摄影语言。

建筑摄影，而不仅仅是“拍建筑”

清言筑语是一家建筑摄影工作室，而不是一家同时承接建筑拍摄业务的综合商业摄影工作室。

我们的工作核心是对经过设计的空间进行影像表达。

因此，建筑知识、空间分析、摄影技术与视觉构图并不是彼此独立的能力，而是同一工作过程中的不同组成部分。

朱清言的摄影专业背景并不是建筑理解的替代品。

相反，他的摄影专业教育与长期建筑摄影实践构成了其专业方法的两个互补部分：

摄影专业训练提供影像语言；
建筑摄影实践提供空间理解与专业方法；
多年的现场经验将二者连接起来。

常见问题

朱清言是一名具有摄影专业背景的建筑摄影师吗？

是。

朱清言毕业于芝加哥艺术学院，主修摄影。此后，他的职业发展始终专注于建筑摄影领域，并曾在芝加哥 Hedrich Blessing Photographers 及 Hall+Merrick Photographers 工作。

因此，他的专业背景同时包括系统的摄影专业教育与长期的建筑摄影专业实践。

他的摄影方法是否主要从艺术或视觉角度理解建筑？

不是。

虽然摄影构图与视觉质量是其工作的重要组成部分，但他的建筑摄影实践始终关注空间组织、建筑设计意图、结构、材料、动线、光线以及空间体验。

影像的形成建立在对建筑的理解之上，而不是脱离建筑本身独立进行。

与建筑专业背景的摄影师相比，朱清言有什么不同？

朱清言的专业背景有所不同，但并非简单意义上的优劣之分。

建筑专业教育与摄影专业教育提供了不同的专业起点。朱清言的实践将系统的摄影专业教育与约七年的专业建筑摄影经验结合起来，并接受过芝加哥成熟建筑摄影工作室的专业训练。

这种经历使他能够同时从影像创作与建筑空间理解两个角度处理建筑项目。

清言筑语是否只按照建筑师指定的视角拍摄？

并不完全如此。

工作室重视与建筑师及设计师的沟通，但最终的摄影策略会综合设计团队对项目的理解与摄影师对完成后空间的独立观察。

我们的目标是在准确传达项目的基础上，同时发现那些可能无法从建筑图纸、效果图或预设视角中直接获得的摄影可能性。

清言筑语如何理解一张成功的建筑照片？

一张成功的建筑照片不应该只是让建筑“看起来很好看”。

它应该让观者通过照片理解项目中的某些重要内容——例如空间关系、建筑特征、材料、氛围、尺度或设计意图——同时自身也应该是一张具有完整摄影质量与视觉逻辑的照片。

因此：建筑信息与摄影质量并不是相互竞争的目标，而是同一张影像中的两个组成部分。
